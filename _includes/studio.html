About Reactive Blend

Founded in 2025 by Ariel Aguilar and Martín Ibarra, Reactive Blend is a game studio born in Rosario, Argentina, where creativity meets retro-modern magic. We're not just developers, we're a dynamic duo of multi-discipline creators.

Ariel mixes code with music and sound design, while Martín dual-wields programming and artistry, crafting 2D/3D worlds with pixel-perfect charm proving that in our studio, even the code has a creative heartbeat.

What Makes Us Unique?

Hybrid Mechanics, Retro Soul: We blend nostalgic aesthetics with modern tools, crafting games that feel like a warm hug from the past with a fresh, tech-savvy twist.

Narrative Adventures: Our passion lies in storytelling, wrapped in indie-sized packages with big imagination.

Collaboration Over Chaos: We let ideas bounce around freely to catch those 'wow' collisions that shape-shift into unique Reactive-Blend adventures.

For Players Who Crave More

We design for curious minds hungry for fresh twists. Think of our games as a love letter to the past, rewritten with modern ink retro vibes, hybrid gameplay, and stories that stick with you.

Behind the Scenes

Tools of the Trade: Blender, Godot, C#, and Reaper fuel our alchemy.

Workflow Philosophy: No rushing here! We let ideas simmer, ensuring every project is a labor of love.
