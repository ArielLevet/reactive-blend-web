pet.exe

ABOUT

Pet.exe is a hybrid 2D-3D horror game where you take care of a mysterious creature. It looks cute, but… don’t be fooled.

As time passes, the pet begins to show strange behavior and your relationship starts to twist. Will you be able to uncover the secrets behind this otherworldly companion?

The game blends suspense and strategy, challenging players to make critical decisions that affect the outcome. Pet.exe is not just horror — it’s an emotional spiral into something... that won’t let you look away.

Making the 3D world:

With the 2D stage defined, we’re now closing the gameplay loop by bringing it into a three-dimensional space.

The 3D location is a seemingly ordinary place that subtly shifts in response to the pet’s mood and the player’s choices.

Through architecture, 3D modeling, lighting and spatial audio, every path and object is designed to convey an immediate emotional tone, from quiet, strange comfort to sudden dread, without relying on mechanical explanation.

Layered puzzles and strategically placed secrets generate emergent interactions that keep players on edge and focused on staying alive.
